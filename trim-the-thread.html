Trim the Thread

Description

Trim the Thread is the debut EP from Quiet Ruin, bringing together five songs written across several years and marking the beginning of the project.

The songs explore control, distraction, isolation and distance.

Reference / Archive Notes

Trim the Thread documents the beginning of Quiet Ruin as a solo project.

Although the songs were written across several years, they came together as the first complete statement of the project's sound and identity.

Many of the ideas introduced on Trim the Thread continued into In the Space Between.
