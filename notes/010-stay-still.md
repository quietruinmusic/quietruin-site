# Stay Still

There are things I could be working on.

Sometimes I feel like I should be getting ahead.

But I've started to wonder what I'm trying to get ahead of.

Before I knew how to turn everything into a finished record, it was just me, the guitar and a loop pedal, coming up with ideas.

I didn't need to know what was going to happen to it.

Time doesn't have to work against that.

An idea can sit for a while.

Maybe not a record.

Maybe not even a song.

I'm just playing.

I'd forgotten how much I like that.

Eventually, when something does start to take shape, I'll want to spend more time with it.

I'll start following the thread to see where it goes.

You can't meet that halfway.

I'm grateful for what music has given me in my life.

It is escapism.

It is immersion.

It is part of my identity.
