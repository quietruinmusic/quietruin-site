# Frequencies

I've always thought there are certain people you just recognise.

Not because they dress a certain way, or listen to the same music, or like the same films.

It's in the way they talk.

It's there in their outlook, what they notice, the way they respond. A certain sense of humour. Maybe a willingness to let the mask slip a little.

I've never had a huge number of friends, but the ones that have lasted tend to be people I share that with.

We don't necessarily have the same tastes. Sometimes they're completely different. But there's usually something underneath it that feels familiar.

A way of seeing.

You're drawn towards certain qualities and repelled by others. Sometimes you know why. Sometimes you don't. You can spend years following those instincts without ever stopping to think about where they came from.

A lot of mine started very early.

Music was always on at home. My dad would stand in one corner of the kitchen with a cigar and a drink in hand, the stereo on in the other, and I'd sit with him while he played tunes and we'd talk about them.

I was taken to gigs young. At school I'd collect music magazines and spend hours in CD shops looking for things I hadn't heard before.

I didn't make a conscious decision to develop a particular taste. I just kept following the thread.

Eventually, you've spent years following those threads, and they become part of you.
