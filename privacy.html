PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Quiet Ruin respects your privacy. This page explains what information may be collected when you use quietruinmusic.com and how it is used.

Information You Provide

If you voluntarily submit your email address through Signal Access, Quiet Ruin may collect and retain that email address.

The email address is used to provide the updates or communications associated with Signal Access.

Quiet Ruin does not sell personal information.

Website Analytics

Quiet Ruin intends to use Google Analytics to understand how the website is used and to help improve the site.

Depending on the implementation and settings, Google Analytics may collect information about things such as:

  • pages visited
  • interactions with the website
  • browser and device information
  • approximate location
  • how visitors arrived at the site

Google Analytics is provided by Google and is subject to Google's own privacy practices.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies, particularly in connection with analytics and embedded third-party services. These technologies can help services remember information about a visit or understand how the website is used.

A detailed cookie inventory will be added when the relevant services and settings have been implemented.

Third-Party Services and Links

Quiet Ruin links to and may embed content from third-party services such as Bandcamp, YouTube, Instagram and Google services.

Those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Quiet Ruin does not control the privacy practices of external websites or services.

How Information Is Used

Information collected through Signal Access is used to provide the communications associated with that service.

Analytics information is used to understand website usage and improve the site.

Data Retention and Removal

Email addresses submitted through Signal Access may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested communications.

Visitors may request removal of their email address or ask questions about their personal information by contacting quietruin@gmail.com.

Security

Quiet Ruin takes reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

New Zealand Privacy

Quiet Ruin operates in New Zealand and aims to handle personal information consistently with applicable New Zealand privacy requirements, including the Privacy Act 2020.

Updates

This Privacy page may be updated when the website, services or information practices change.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests:
